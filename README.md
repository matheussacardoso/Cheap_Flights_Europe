# ✈️ Cheap_Flights_Europe

Got a bunch of bridge days coming up and an itch to explore Europe without spending your entire paycheck or your sanity? 

If you've ever lived in Europe with vacation days and public holiday gaps, you know the drill: every time you want to catch a quick getaway, you end up stuck manually searching six different low-cost airline tabs every single morning. 

This app automates that entire process. It scans budget airlines in the background to find cheap, direct weekend packages tailored specifically around optimal long-weekend travel windows.

---

## 🚀 What This App Delivers

* **Custom "Long Weekend" Presets:** Target high-value trip patterns like Thursday–Monday or Friday–Tuesday to maximize your bridge days without wasting unnecessary leave.
* **100% Direct Flights Only:** Layovers on a 4-day trip ruin the vibe. The app automatically discards multi-stop flights—if it isn't direct, you won't see it.
* **Custom Time Windows:** Avoid 5:00 AM departures on workdays or landing at midnight after public transit has stopped running. Filter flights by your preferred departure and arrival time frames.
* **Flexible Price Guardrails:** Set price caps for total round-trips or evaluate outbound and inbound legs separately so you stay strictly within budget.
* **Multi-Airline Aggregator:** Queries all major low-cost European carriers in one pass:
  * **Ryanair**
  * **easyJet**
  * **Wizz Air**
  * **Vueling**
  * **Eurowings**
  * **Norwegian**

---

## 📊 Manual Searching vs. Automated Finder

| Feature | The Manual Way | With This App |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search Effort** | Opening 6+ browser tabs every morning | One automated scan running silently |
| **Date Logic** | Re-keying date ranges one combination at a time | Batch scanning pre-configured Thursday–Monday windows |
| **Price Tracking** | Trying to remember yesterday's fares | Instant alerts when prices fall into your budget range |
| **Route Filtering** | Sorting through 14-hour layover options | Hard-coded to filter for direct flights only |

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Set your home origin airport.**
2. **Choose your date pattern** (e.g., Thursday to Monday or Friday to Tuesday).
3. **Define your flight preferences** (preferred hours, max price per leg or total).
4. **Let it run:** The tool scans low-cost carriers and surfaces matching flight bundles ready to book.
